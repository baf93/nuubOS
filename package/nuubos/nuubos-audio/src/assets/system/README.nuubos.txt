nuubOS System Sound Set v5

Original synthetic PCM assets created for nuubOS by
tools/sounds/make-system-sounds.py (pure synthesis, reproducible). No
third-party samples are used. They are distributed under the same MIT
license as the nuubos-audio package.

One warm, low family (fundamentals D3-D5, felt-mallet tones, gentle
low-pass, a small shared room) in D major. Forward gestures rise, back and
power off fall; power and launch cues are built on the nuubOS motif
(D - F# - A - D).

Navigation Sounds:
- navigation.wav: a short soft wooden tick
- select.wav: rising fifth
- back.wav: falling fifth
- quick-settings.wav: Quick Menu / Settings open (air + rising shimmer)
- launch.wav: a game starts (the motif rushing up)
Notification Sounds:
- error.wav: warnings and errors (soft falling semitone)
- screenshot.wav: screenshot saved (soft shutter)
Power Sounds:
- boot.wav: the motif over a warm pad
- restart.wav: the motif turning back on itself
- poweroff.wav: the motif falling to rest

48 kHz / mono / signed 16-bit PCM WAV.
