nuubOS built-in profile pictures
================================

The 24 built-in avatars are rendered with DiceBear (https://www.dicebear.com/,
avatar styles 9.x) from fixed seeds, as a round avatar on a pastel background, and rasterized
to 192x192 PNG. The artwork is otherwise unmodified. Regenerate with
tools/avatars/generate.sh in the nuubOS repository.

01-08  "Big Smile" style (seeds Felix, Aneka, Mimi, Sasha, Nova, Pixel, Kiwi, Luna)
       Design: "Custom Avatar" by Ashley Seo (http://www.ashleyseo.com/)
       Source: https://www.figma.com/community/file/881358461963645496
       License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

09-16  "Fun Emoji" style (seeds Felix, Mimi, Sasha, Lilly, Leo, Rocket, Juno, Otto)
       Design: "Fun Emoji Set" by Davis Uche (https://www.instagram.com/davedirect3/)
       Source: https://www.figma.com/community/file/968125295144990435
       License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

17-24  "Open Peeps" style (seeds Felix, Aneka, Lilly, Nova, Pixel, Mochi, Kiwi, Otto)
